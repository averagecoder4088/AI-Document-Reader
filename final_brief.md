## SRVCABLE: Research Brief (as of 23 September 2026)

### Snapshot
- Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires and conductors [S3].  
- Q1 FY27 revenue was ₹1,248 cr, up 18 % YoY; profit after tax ₹82 cr, up 5.9 % YoY [S3].  
- EBITDA ₹140 cr, giving an EBITDA margin of 11.2 % (reconciles with the ₹1,248 cr revenue) [S3].  
- Order book stood at ₹3,900 cr, a 23.8 % increase YoY [S3]; shares fell to ₹1,012 on 9 Aug 2026 (price not current) [S1].

### Bull case
- Institutional ownership improved: FII holding rose to 11.8 % (up 2.6 pts) and DII to 14.6 % (up 1.9 pts) since June 2025 [S8].  
- Promoter-pledge fell sharply to 4.1 % of promoter holding (down 7.4 pts) from 11.5 % a year earlier [S8].  
- The new Bharuch plant adds about 30 % to cable capacity and is on track for commercial production in Q3 FY27 [S3].  
- Data-centre cables now account for ~9 % of domestic revenue; management expects this share to double in two years [S2].  
- Bharuch capex of ₹620 cr is ~70 % spent and is funded mainly through internal accruals with limited term debt [S2].

### Bear case
- GST demand of ₹46.3 cr (incl. interest/penalty) equals roughly 56 % of Q1 FY27 profit; management says it does not expect a material impact [S7].  
- Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, indicating slower cash collection [S2].  
- Profit growth lags sales: revenue +18 % YoY but EBITDA only +2.6 % YoY [S3].  
- Operating margin fell 170 bps to 11.2 % YoY [S1].  
- Copper, the main raw material, rose nearly 14 % during the quarter, pressuring input costs [S1].

### Open questions
- Q1 revenue discrepancy: S1 reports ₹1,428 cr, S3 reports ₹1,248 cr. Using S1’s figure, EBITDA ₹140 cr ÷ revenue ₹1,428 cr = 9.8 %, which does not match the stated 11.2 % margin; S3’s figure yields 11.2 % and reconciles, so S3 is the reliable source [S1][S3].  
- The GST demand of ₹46.3 cr is about 56 % of Q1 profit, but no independent verification of the company’s “no material impact” claim is available [S7].  
- No up-to-date share price, valuation multiples, or cash-flow statements are provided; the only price (₹1,012) is dated 9 Aug 2026 and may be outdated [S1].
